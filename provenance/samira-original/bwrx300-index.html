Plant Systems
System What it does In this simulator
Control rod drive Moves rods; hydraulic scram inserts all rods fast. The computer moves rods from the lever. SCRAM button. In free play, set Rod control to Manual to move rods yourself.
ARI Backup rod insertion by a diverse pneumatic path when rods stick. ARI button forces rods in when ATWS occurs.
Standby liquid control (SLC) Injects liquid sodium pentaborate poison to shut the reactor down when rods fail. SLC switch with a finite tank. Needs AC power.
Reactor water cleanup (RWCU) Filters impurities and removes injected boron from the water. Cleanup slider. Higher rate clears boron faster.
Feedwater and condensate Returns condensed water to the vessel to hold water level. Auto or manual feed, level setpoint slider, standby RFP pump.
Main steam, MSIVs, relief valves Steam drives the turbine; MSIVs isolate it; relief valves vent to the suppression pool and ADS depressurises. MSIV toggle, automatic safety relief valves (SRV), ADS button.
Turbine, bypass, condenser Generates electricity; bypass dumps excess steam directly to the condenser, cooled by Lake Ontario. Bypass auto/manual slider, circulating water (CW) pumps (0-3).
Isolation condenser (IC) Passive decay heat removal with no pumps or power. Sits in an open pool above containment; steam rises, condenses, and drains by gravity. Auto or off. Drawn on the roof. Click to inspect detail in new window.
RCIC Steam-turbine pump that keeps water level up using reactor steam and DC battery power only. Off / auto / on. Runs during blackout if batteries hold.
High-pressure injection (HPCF) Electric motor pump that injects water into the vessel at full operating pressure. Off / auto / on. Needs AC power.
RHR Low-pressure injection (LPCI), pool cooling, and shutdown cooling; rejects heat to the lake. Mode selector: Off, Pool, Shutdown (SDC), LPCI.
Suppression pool & containment Condenses steam from relief valves and contains steam pressure. Pool temperature and containment pressure monitoring.
Filtered containment vent Vents excess pressure through a water scrubber and metal filter to protect containment integrity. Vent switch with release score penalty.
Electric power: Diesels, batteries, GTG Keeps pumps, valves, and digital controls powered when offsite grid is lost. EDG auto/on, GTG mobile generator start, battery percentage bar.
Alternate water injection A mobile fire engine or portable pump injects water into the vessel at low pressure (<0.9 MPa). Alternate injection switch.

Systems Reference

Isolation Condenser: Passive gravity drain, 7 days water pool on roof.

Natural Circulation: Driven by buoyancy in tall chimney, no pumps.

Boron (SLC): Fast chemical shutdown if rods fail to insert (ATWS).

Filtered Vent: Scrubber retains 99.9% of particulates during overpressure.
